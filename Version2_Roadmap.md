# StateCraft - Version 2.0 Roadmap

This document tracks all the polish, quality-of-life (QoL), and advanced feature ideas that are slated for Version 2 of StateCraft, allowing us to stay focused on completing the core functional loops for Version 1.

## Military & Defense

### Contextual Construction Intelligence
*When building new Military Bases, Training Academies, or Defense Factories, provide the player with dynamic regional intelligence to inform their decision.*
- **Regional Context Panel**: Display stats for the selected region (e.g., Infrastructure Level, Security/Stability, Industrial Capacity, Available Land) inside the construction modal.
- **Why**: Helps the player understand the strategic tradeoffs of building in an unstable region vs a highly developed one.

### Facility Specialization Explanations
*Remove guesswork by clearly detailing what a facility specialization provides.*
- **Dynamic Info Tooltips/Panels**: When a player selects a specialization (e.g., "Tank Factory" or "Naval Vessels"), dynamically update the UI to show exactly what is produced (Main Battle Tanks, APCs, Recovery Vehicles) and what resources are required to run it (Steel Supply, Power, Industrial Capacity).

## General UI/UX Polish

- **Enhanced Visual Hierarchy**: Continue refining button weights, colors, and iconography (like the bright green `🔨 COMMENCE CONSTRUCTION` button) across all dashboards to guide the player's eye to primary actions.
- **Dynamic Tooltips**: Add hover tooltips across complex stats (like Threat Levels, Readiness, or Upkeep) to explain the underlying math to the player without cluttering the screen.

*Note: Feel free to add new ideas to this list as we continue building V1!*
