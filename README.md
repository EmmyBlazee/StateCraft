StateCraft is a geopolitical grand strategy and nation simulator.

Core Systems & Features:
Domestic Politics & Governance: Approval, regional stability, insurgency, factions, elections, policies.
Comprehensive Economic Engine: Sovereign budget, debt, taxation, inflation, commodities, industry construction, and power grid simulation.
Military & Defense: Armed forces branches (Army, Navy, Air Force, RDF), postures, military bases, weapons procurement, and dynamic war simulation.
Intelligence & Recon: Threat assessment, surveillance, covert ops, and border monitoring.
Diplomacy & Living World: Bilateral relations matrix, trade agreements, world tension, live news ticker, and dispatch inbox.
Featured Nations & Scenarios: Briefings covering playable powers (Nigeria, Ghana, Ethiopia, South Africa, Egypt, Kenya, Morocco, DR Congo, Tanzania, Angola).
Architecture & Technical Stack: Unity UI Toolkit (UXML/USS), ScriptableObject data layer, and event-driven architecture.
Getting Started Guide: Prerequisites and setup instructions.
